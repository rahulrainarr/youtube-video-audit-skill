# YouTube Video Audit Report Generator

## Overview
Comprehensive YouTube video audit skill that analyzes content across 10 critical dimensions and generates actionable, business-focused recommendations. Designed for marketing teams, content creators, and business stakeholders.

---

## When to Use

✓ Pre-publication video review  
✓ Performance optimization for existing videos  
✓ Competitive benchmarking  
✓ Content strategy development  
✓ SEO improvement initiatives  
✓ Engagement and retention analysis  
✓ Multi-video content audits  
✓ Executive/leadership positioning review  

---

## Input Requirements

### Essential Information
- **Video URL** (YouTube link) OR **Video Title + Description + Transcript**
- **Target Audience** (who should watch this)
- **Business Objective** (what you want to achieve)

### Recommended Information
- **Video Duration** (MM:SS format)
- **Channel Name**
- **Publication Date**
- **Full Transcript or Captions**
- **Video Summary** (1-2 sentences)

### Optional but Valuable
- **Production Quality Notes** (lighting, audio, editing)
- **Analytics Data** (views, watch time, CTR, engagement)
- **Thumbnail Description** or Image
- **Tags/Hashtags**
- **Competitor Videos** (for benchmarking)
- **Known Issues** (problems you're aware of)

### Missing Data Notice
Incomplete data is fine — the audit proceeds with available information. Analysis will:
- Continue with what you provide
- Clearly state assumptions made
- Flag limitations due to missing data
- Suggest critical data to gather

## Output: Report Structure

### 1. Executive Summary
- **Overall Assessment** (2-3 sentences)
- **Key Strengths** (top 3 with examples)
- **Key Gaps** (top 3 with business impact)
- **Priority Quick Wins** (immediate improvements)
- **Overall Audit Score** (0-100)

### 2. Video Information Reviewed
Summary of source material, analytical data available, and metadata.

### 3. Overall Audit Score (0-100)
Single score with brief explanation.

### 4. Detailed Scorecard (Each 1-10)
- **Content Clarity** — Message effectiveness and comprehension
- **Hook Strength** — First 15-30 seconds impact
- **SEO Optimization** — Title, description, keywords, metadata
- **Thumbnail Effectiveness** — Visual appeal and CTR potential
- **Audience Retention** — Structure, pacing, engagement
- **Engagement Potential** — Comments, shares, likes likelihood
- **Brand Alignment** — Business identity consistency
- **Technical Quality** — Audio, video, editing, captions
- **CTA Effectiveness** — Call-to-action clarity
- **Business Impact** — Contribution to stated objective

### 5. Detailed Audit Findings

**Content Quality Analysis**
- Message clarity and structure
- Hook effectiveness
- Information depth and delivery
- Viewer journey and pacing

**YouTube SEO Analysis**
- Title optimization for search
- Description keyword usage
- Chapter markers and timestamps
- Search intent alignment

**Thumbnail Analysis**
- Visual hierarchy and clarity
- Mobile readability
- Emotional appeal
- CTR optimization potential

**Engagement Strategy**
- Comment-prompting elements
- CTA placement and clarity
- Interaction triggers
- Subscription incentives

**Audience Retention**
- Opening hook strength
- Pacing and rhythm
- Complexity balance
- Drop-off risk points

**Brand & Positioning**
- Brand consistency
- Professional tone
- Trust signal strength
- Differentiation clarity

**Technical & Production**
- Audio quality and clarity
- Video resolution and quality
- Editing and transitions
- Caption/subtitle quality

**Compliance & Risk**
- Copyright concerns
- Factual accuracy
- Brand safety
- Required disclosures

### 6. Key Strengths (Top 3-5)
Specific examples with business impact.

### 7. Key Gaps (Top 3-5)
Issues identified with severity assessment.

### 8. Improvement Recommendations

**Quick Wins (0-7 days)**
- High impact, low effort improvements
- Specific actionable steps
- Expected outcomes

**Medium-Term (2-4 weeks)**
- Moderate effort, significant impact
- Implementation guidance
- Resource requirements

**Strategic (1-3 months)**
- Significant effort, major impact
- Long-term business value
- Phased approach

Each recommendation includes: What, Why, How, Expected Outcome

### 9. Optimized Metadata

- **Improved Title Options** (3 variants: keyword, benefit, curiosity-focused)
- **Improved Description** (keyword-optimized rewrite)
- **Suggested Keywords** (5-10 search terms with context)
- **Suggested Hashtags** (5-8 relevant)
- **Suggested Chapters** (if timestamps available)

### 10. Engagement Improvement Strategy

- **Optimized CTA** (More compelling, specific)
- **Suggested Pinned Comment** (Community-building)
- **Engagement Questions** (Comment prompts)
- **Community/Series Ideas** (Follow-up content)

### 11. Thumbnail Improvement Concept

- Visual description of stronger thumbnail
- Specific design improvements
- Applied design principles
- Expected CTR impact

### 12. Competitive Positioning (if applicable)

- Competitive comparison
- Gaps vs. competitors
- Differentiation opportunities
- Strategies to outperform

### 13. Risk & Compliance Assessment

- Copyright or claim risks
- Factual accuracy concerns
- Brand safety issues
- Required disclosures

### 14. Final Action Plan

**Prioritized checklist with:**
- Action item
- Owner/Responsibility
- Effort level (Quick/Medium/High)
- Expected impact (Low/Medium/High)
- Timeline and dependencies

## Scoring Framework

### Dimension Scoring (1-10)
- **1-3** = Significant issues, major revision needed
- **4-6** = Room for improvement, moderate issues
- **7-8** = Solid foundation, optimization opportunities
- **9-10** = Excellent, best-in-class

### Overall Score (0-100)
Average of 10 key dimensions, with context-based weighting.

### Score Interpretation
- **0-40** = Comprehensive revision recommended
- **41-60** = Good foundation, meaningful optimization needed
- **61-75** = Solid video, incremental improvements valuable
- **76-90** = Strong video, refinements maximize performance
- **91-100** = Excellent video, minimal improvements needed

---

## Key Principles

1. **Business-Focused** — All recommendations tied to your stated objective
2. **Specific, Not Generic** — Tailored to your video, not boilerplate
3. **Actionable** — Concrete "how-to" guidance included
4. **Prioritized** — Highest-impact improvements first
5. **Realistic** — Feasible within typical constraints
6. **Data-Driven** — Uses analytics and industry benchmarks
7. **Transparent** — Clear assumptions and limitations noted
8. **Clear** — Professional, accessible language

## How the Skill Works

### Phase 1: Information Gathering
- Collects or extracts video metadata
- Reviews available analytics
- Identifies focus areas

### Phase 2: Multi-Dimensional Analysis
- Analyzes 10 key dimensions
- Scores each dimension (1-10)
- Identifies patterns and themes

### Phase 3: Competitive Context
- Benchmarks against competitor videos (if provided)
- Identifies differentiation opportunities
- Assesses relative positioning

### Phase 4: Recommendations
- Prioritizes improvements by impact/effort
- Groups into quick-win, medium, and strategic tracks
- Provides specific, actionable guidance

### Phase 5: Deliverables
- Generates comprehensive report (3,000-5,000 words)
- Creates optimized metadata variants
- Develops action plan with timeline

## Limitations & Assumptions

### What the Audit Covers
- Content quality and structure
- SEO and discoverability
- Engagement and retention strategies
- Technical production quality
- Brand alignment and positioning
- Business objective alignment

### What It Cannot Do
- Predict exact future performance (based on YouTube algorithm)
- Replace A/B testing (recommendations are best-practice informed)
- Account for influencer status or channel authority
- Assess audience sentiment in depth (limited by available data)
- Guarantee specific engagement metrics

### Quality Depends On
- Transcript or content details provided
- Completeness of description and context
- Clarity of business objective
- Available analytics data
- Specificity of target audience definition
