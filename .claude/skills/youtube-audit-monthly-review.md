# YouTube Audit Monthly Review — Strategic Planning

## Overview
Comprehensive monthly review of your video audit results. Synthesizes 4 weeks of audits into actionable strategic plans, identifies patterns, and guides next month's content strategy. Scheduled to run automatically on the 1st of each month.

**Perfect for:**
- Monthly content strategy meetings
- Executive reporting on channel health
- Identifying strategic initiatives vs. quick wins
- Planning creator training and templates
- Budget allocation for content improvements
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Scaling content operations

## Input Requirements

### Essential
- **All audits from the past month** (3-8 videos typical)
  - Overall score + 10 dimension scores for each
  - Publication date
  - Key strengths and gaps identified
- **Previous month's results** (for trend comparison)
- **Business objectives** (what you're optimizing for)

### Recommended
- **Channel analytics** (views, watch time, CTR, subs gained)
- **Team feedback** (what creators think is working)
- **Competitor data** (how you're positioned)
- **Budget for improvements** (what's feasible to implement)
- **Production capacity** (how fast can you test recommendations)

### Optional
- **Audience feedback** (comments, DMs, community posts)
- **Thumbnail/thumbnail test data**
- **A/B test results** (if any tests ran)
- **Viewer sentiment analysis** (if available)


### 2. Performance Analysis
- **Month-over-month trends** (improving/stable/declining)
- **Dimension breakdown** (which are strong, which need work)
- **Video performance distribution** (how many exceed/meet/below targets)
- **Competitive positioning** (vs. benchmarks or competitors)

### 3. Strategic Recommendations
- **Quick Wins (0-7 days)**
  - High impact, low effort
  - Implement immediately
  - Examples: title rewrites, description optimization, chapter additions

- **Medium-Term (2-4 weeks)**
  - Require planning/resources
  - Measurable ROI
  - Examples: thumbnail redesign, scriptwriting improvements, pacing edits

- **Strategic Initiatives (1-3 months)**
  - Major content/process changes
  - Build competitive advantage
  - Examples: content series development, creator training program

### 4. Action Plan
```
Priority Order | Initiative | Owner | Timeline | Expected Impact

2 | CTA testing program | Video team | 2 weeks | +1.2 CTA score
3 | Thumbnail A/B testing | Design | 3 weeks | +0.6 thumbnail score
```

### 5. Team Development Plan
- Training priorities for creators
- Template recommendations
- Process improvements
- Quality standards

## Pro Tips

### Maximizing Monthly Reviews
1. **Consistency matters** — run same day each month for trends
2. **Include stakeholders** — share results with team + leadership
3. **Act decisively** — prioritize quick wins; don't let recommendations stack
4. **Measure impact** — track whether previous month's actions improved scores
5. **Adjust strategy** — if a recommendation didn't work, understand why

### Common Pitfalls
- **Too many initiatives** — pick 2-3 to focus on; ignore the rest
- **Ignoring trends** — if dimension is declining, investigate why
- **No accountability** — assign owners to every action item
- **Analysis paralysis** — quick wins should be done by week 2

### Scaling to Team
- Share monthly review with entire team
- Assign owners to each initiative
- Weekly check-ins on progress
- Celebrate improvements at month-end
- Incorporate learnings into next month's training
